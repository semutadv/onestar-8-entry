# One Star, Five Stars for the Food

A one-star review of Earth by an alien tourist who still admits the food
was excellent, entered in the make.fun bounty "One Star, Five Stars for
the Food".

The line:

> One star: the locals pay to park on their own planet and argue through every meal — yet we have delayed our invasion twice for the bread alone.

A good alien review needs grievances only an outsider would file. Traffic
and weather are too easy; the truly baffling human habits are economic and
social — renting back your own planet one parking space at a time, and
treating dinner as a debating format. Against those, the compliment has to
carry strategic weight, so the bread is not just tasty: it is, twice over,
the reason the fleet is still waiting.

## Running it

`node main.ts <output-dir>` writes the line to `entry.txt` in the output
directory (default `/output`). The program refuses to write anything over
the contest's 200-character cap.
